What the app stores on your device

To let you keep playing where you left off, Tintly saves the following locally on your device only:

  • your current level and progress toward unlocks,
  • coins earned and items you have unlocked or purchased with in-game coins,
  • your remaining undos and extra bottles,
  • your customization choices (colour palette, background, bottle style).

This information never leaves your device and is not linked to you as a person. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or any account credentials. Tintly has no user accounts and no cloud save.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tintly shows optional rewarded video ads — for example, to refill your undos or extra bottles. Ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, where you have allowed it),
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed to completion, or clicked), and diagnostics.

Google uses this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where required by law (for example in the EU/EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can decline; non-personalized ads may still be served.

You can limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings: on Android under Settings › Google › Ads (reset or delete your advertising ID), on iOS under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Children

Tintly is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google AdMob, for the advertising purposes described above.
